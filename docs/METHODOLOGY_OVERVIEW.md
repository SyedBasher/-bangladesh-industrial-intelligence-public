# Methodology overview — public summary

Bangladesh Industrial & Factory Intelligence is being developed as an evidence-based view of Bangladesh's industrial economy.

## Establishment backbone

The public Department of Inspection for Factories and Establishments (DIFE/LIMA) register is the planned principal establishment reference. An establishment is not automatically equivalent to an organization, company, reported plant, manufacturing line or product.

## Additional evidence

Subject to lawful access, licensing and verification, additional source layers may include EPB, BGMEA, BKMEA, BEPZA, Department of Environment and corporate disclosures. Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics administrative geography provides an explicit reference for place-name resolution. Exposure data may supply location context, but district or upazila evidence is never silently represented as site-specific evidence.

## Evidence and uncertainty

The system is designed to preserve source authority, date/vintage, retrieval/version information, scope and calculation lineage in protected storage. Official observations, external statements, analytical calculations and tentative entity links must remain distinguishable. An unsupported value is reported as unknown, not guessed.

## Coverage and validation

Validation sample statistics cannot automatically be presented as national totals. Data presence/coverage is different from the incidence, prevalence or probability of the reported condition. Real-world extraction and matching must be calibrated against independently reviewed evidence before publication.

## Current public status

The interface is **a synthetic development preview, not a live data service**. Future longitudinal annual-report, industrial investment and technology disclosure modules remain subject to additional engineering, validation and human review.
